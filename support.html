Your prayer list

Where is my list kept?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

I don't get the morning reminder

Check Settings in Intercede (the gear on Today) that the reminder is on and the time is right, and that notifications for Intercede are allowed in the iPhone's Settings app.

Church prayer feeds

How do I join my church?

Open the Church tab, sign in with Apple, agree to the feed guidelines, and enter the invite code from one of your church's leaders.

How do I start a feed for my church?

Church › sign in › Start a feed for your church. You become its first leader and get an invite code to share. New requests wait for a leader's approval until you change that.

A request shouldn't be there

Tap … on it and choose Report. The church's leaders see it right away and can remove it. You can also block its author: you won't see their requests, and they aren't told. If it is serious or a leader is the problem, write to us and we will act within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

In Intercede: Settings › Church feed › Delete church feed account. Everything you shared in church feeds is deleted at once. Your own prayer list stays on your iPhone.
