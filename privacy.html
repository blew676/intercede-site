Privacy policy

Updated 14 November 2026

Intercede is a prayer list for iPhone, made by Brandon Lewis. This page says what the app keeps, where, and who can see it.

Your prayer list stays on your iPhone

The people and requests on your list, your notes, your answered prayers and your reminders are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

Church prayer feeds (only if you join one)

A church feed is optional. If you sign in to one, the app sends some things to our database so the members of your church can pray together:

  • Your account. You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us a private identifier for you. We do not ask Apple for your email address.
  • Your name, as you choose to write it. Your church sees it on the requests you share with your name.
  • Your churches: which feeds you belong to and whether you are a leader there.
  • What you share: each request's words, whether it is anonymous, and who may see it (the whole church or its leaders only).
  • Prayers, reports and blocks: that you prayed for a request (counted, and shown as a number), requests you report and why, and authors you block.

Who can see what

  • Members of a church see that church's approved requests and the names on requests shared with a name.
  • Leaders of a church also see requests waiting for approval, requests shared for leaders only, the members list, and reported requests with the reasons given, never who reported them.
  • Anonymous means anonymous. No one in the church is shown who shared an anonymous request, leaders included. We keep the link between the request and your account only so you can manage it and so abuse can be dealt with.
  • No one outside a church can see anything in its feed.

What we never do

No advertising. No analytics. No tracking across apps or websites. We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Where it is kept

Church feeds are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the Americas. Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS).

How long, and deleting it

What you share stays until you delete it, a leader removes it, the church's feed is deleted, or you delete your account. Requests stop showing in the feed after 30 days unless you keep them up.

Delete your account at any time in the app: Settings › Church feed › Delete church feed account. Your account and everything you shared are deleted from our database at once. Your own prayer list on your iPhone is not affected.

Children

Intercede is not directed to children under 13, and church feeds are not meant for them.

Changes and contact
