Intercede

Remember the people you pray for, and the prayers God answers.

Your prayer list

The people in your life and what they've asked you to pray for. Each morning, at the time you meet with the Lord, Intercede reminds you who is on your list today, and Pray now walks you through them one at a time. Your list stays on your iPhone.

Pray together as a church

Join your church's prayer feed with an invite code from a leader. Share a request with your name, anonymously, or with your leaders only. Tap I prayed so they know they're not alone.
